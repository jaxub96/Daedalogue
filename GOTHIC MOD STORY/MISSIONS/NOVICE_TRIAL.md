Once all three are completed, you will do the final trial.
The final trial will lead you to an archeological excavation site. There, the archeologists under supervision of priest *[[Moneo]]* are searching for arcane knowledge, and historical evidence of The Wanderer. 

The archeologists have been scattered. Find them, and help them gather materials and exit. 

You find that the archeologists have been killed. Recover their journals, and clear out the site. 

Find the main chamber and discover the treasure. [[MASK_1]]

Get back to Moneo, report to him. He will send you back to Nestor, where you complete the final trial.
