# Chapter 1

## **[[PRISON]]**

- [[HERO]] wakes up in a prison cell.
- guard *[[Arideus]]* comes to your cell.
- choose face, head, body, name.
- exit [[PRISON]] dungeon.
- [[Kilian]] is a neighbour prisoner.
- Calls you out when you exit. Asks if you can vouch for his release
	- Yes, I will try 
		- +1 to [[MORALITY]]
	- No, I don't know you
		- -1 to [[MORALITY]]
>[!TIP] [[FAVOR]]
- [[Arideus]] takes you to commander [[NPC/Malachus]]
- [[NPC/Malachus]] signs you off, gives back your inventory
- get a [[dagger]], some gold and a [[mysterious note]]

> [!TIP] [[MYSTERY_GIFT]]

- ask [[NPC/Malachus]] about items:
	- "Yes they are mine" (lie)
		- -1 to [[MORALITY]]
		- you claim the items
	- "These items aren't mine" (true)
		- +1 to [[MORALITY]]
		- ask about items
		- [[NPC/Malachus]] says they are under your name
		- not his business.
		- suggests to sell [[dagger]], or donate to Temple
- *[[NPC/Malachus]]* directs you to a nearby **[[SICCA_TEMPLE]]** for work/shelter
## **[[ROAD]]**
- A robber named *[[Persius]]* stops you, and tries to rob you of the [[dagger]]
    - give [[dagger]]
	    - +1 to [[MORALITY]]
        - the robber takes your [[dagger]]
        - you will have to recover it later
    - fight back
	    - -1 to [[MORALITY]]
        - kill [[Persius]]
        - find a [[clue]] on his body
> [!NOTE] [[MYSTERY_GIFT]]

- walk the road from **[[PRISON]]**
- eventually find **[[SICCA_TEMPLE]]**
- temple guard *[[Varus]]* stops you
>[!TIP] [[TEMPLE_ENTRY]]

## **[[SICCA_TEMPLE]]**

- *[[Nestor]]* asks you of your background
- gives you some work in the temple
>[!TIP] [[TEMPLE_WORK]]
- gives you a room for a night.

Offers you [[NOVICE_TRAINING]]

[[Nestor]]: 
"This [[dagger]] looks like a necromancer item known as a "[[Harvester]]". According to legends, killing someone with a [[Harvester]] extracts their soul. Necromancers used souls to magically enhance objects, making them more powerful in battle, but in exchange corrupting their own soul, for each soul they harvest. This practice has been forbidden by the Mage Council a century ago, and was believed to be extinct. "


HERO:
"What happened to the harvesters?"

Nestor:
"Some were destroyed by the Mage Counsel, others have gone missing. If this dagger really is a harvester, it should be destroyed at once."

HERO: 
"What does that have to do with me?"

Nestor: 
"The dagger is in your possession. Whether you want it or not, you are responsible for it. If it's ever used for evil, by you or someone else, the Custodians will hunt you down and punish you."

HERO
"Custodians?"

Nestor
"An ancient order, serving the Sicca. They protect the realm against demonic cults, root out heresy and persecute practitioners of forbidden arts. The latter role has been dormant for many decades of course."

HERO:
"Then what should I do? Can I leave it to you?"

Nestor:
"Absolutely kurwa not. I can't let the dagger corrupt the sacred temple of Sicca. Take it to the Court Wizard, [[Lazarus]]. As a member of the Mage Counsel, he will know what to do."



>[!tip] [[WIZARD'S COUNSEL]]

assigns [[CULT_DUNGEON]] if not done.

go for the first expedition and kill some [[Cultists]].
their leader is a mask owner.
you take the mask
a demon appears and kills your expedition 
you return to give the news.
