# Chapter 1

## **[[PRISON]]**

- [[HERO]] wakes up in a prison cell.
- guard *[[Arideus]]* comes to your cell.
- choose face, head, body, name.
- exit [[PRISON]] dungeon.
- [[Kilian]] is a neighbour prisoner.
- Calls you out when you exit. Asks if you can vouch for his release
	- Yes, I will try 
		- +1 to [[MORALITY]]
	- No, I don't know you
		- -1 to [[MORALITY]]
>[!TIP] [[FAVOR]]
- [[Arideus]] takes you to commander [[Malachus]]
- [[Malachus]] signs you off, gives back your inventory
- get a [[dagger]], some gold and a [[mysterious note]]

> [!TIP] [[MYSTERY_GIFT]]

- ask [[Malachus]] about items:
	- "Yes they are mine" (lie)
		- -1 to [[MORALITY]]
		- you claim the items
	- "These items aren't mine" (true)
		- +1 to [[MORALITY]]
		- ask about items
		- [[Malachus]] says they are under your name
		- not his business.
		- suggests to sell [[dagger]], or donate to Temple
- *[[Malachus]]* directs you to a nearby **[[SICCA_TEMPLE]]** for work/shelter
## **[[ROAD]]**
- A robber named *[[Persius]]* stops you, and tries to rob you of the [[dagger]]
    - give [[dagger]]
	    - +1 to [[MORALITY]]
        - the robber takes your [[dagger]]
        - you will have to recover it later
    - fight back
	    - -1 to [[MORALITY]]
        - kill [[Persius]]
        - find a [[clue]] on his body
> [!NOTE] [[MYSTERY_GIFT]]

- walk the road from **[[PRISON]]**
- eventually find **[[SICCA_TEMPLE]]**
- temple guard *[[Varus]]* stops you
>[!TIP] [[TEMPLE_ENTRY]]

## **[[SICCA_TEMPLE]]**

- *[[Nestor]]* asks you of your background
- gives you some work in the temple
>[!TIP] [[TEMPLE_WORK]]
- gives you a room for a night.

Offers you [[NOVICE_TRAINING]]


tell him about [[dagger]]
Dagger is a necromancer item. Killing someone with it extracts their soul. Souls can be used in Alchemy. Dagger corrupts the user, more for each soul. Necromancy is forbidden. 

- The dagger should be destroyed
- How? Can I leave it with you?
- No. Bring it to the court wizard *[[Lazarus]]*. He will know what to do.
- How to get to [[Lazarus]]? 
- You will have to be admitted to the court somehow.
>[!tip] [[WIZARD'S COUNSEL]]

assigns [[CULT_DUNGEON]] if not done.

Sends you to the **[[CITY]]** to be recruited to the [[Custodians]]



## **[[CITY]]**
the [[Custodians]] are looking for volunteer workers to hunt the [[Cultists]]
you can join if you're strong enough.
First mission - interrogate a captured cultist about their hideout.

go for the first expedition and kill some [[Cultists]].
their leader is a mask owner.
you take the mask
a demon appears and kills your expedition 
you return to give the news.


## **[[SANCTUARY]]**

to enter you need five masks
each mask has a power
you need to find mask-owners and kill them
return the dagger to the sanctuary
