- The player starts in a prison cell.

- A guard unlocks the door.

- “You’re being released.”

- The guard either does not know why or has been ordered not to say. The player is brought before the prison warden, who returns their confiscated possessions.

- Among them are two things the player has never seen before: an ornamental dagger and a sealed letter.

- The letter reads:
“You’re free. Don’t thank me.
As long as you remain useful, you can count on my grace.
I leave you my dagger. It will help you to get ahead.
Return it to me when it becomes a burden.”

- No signature.

- The warden refuses to discuss it. As far as the prison records are concerned, the order for the player’s release came through legitimate channels.

- The gates open.

- The player is free.

- Outside the prison, the player has no employment and nowhere particular to go. A guard or traveler mentions the nearby village of [[NIDAROS]], built around an old temple. Pilgrims and travelers regularly pass through, and the temple sometimes provides food, shelter, and simple work.

- The player can explore freely, but [[NIDAROS]] becomes the natural first destination.

- When the player reaches the village, a crowd has gathered outside the temple.

- An execution is underway.

- The condemned man is [[Elias]], a local stonemason. He has been convicted of practicing forbidden rites, desecrating temple ground, and attempting to spread heretical teachings.

- Before the executioner kills him, he looks toward the temple and shouts:

- Some villagers laugh. Others become visibly uncomfortable.

- The priests order him silenced.

- He is executed.

- Afterward, the player approaches [[Loras]], one of the temple guards, asking about work and shelter.

- [[Loras]] directs the player inside to [[Nestor]], an elderly priest responsible for much of the temple’s mundane administration.

- [[Nestor]] is initially uninterested in the player’s history. The temple needs hands, and the player needs food.

- He offers a simple arrangement: do some work and receive a bed and meals.

- The first task is deliberately ordinary.

- A young novice named [[Basil]] recently abandoned the temple without permission. His small room needs to be emptied so another novice can use it.

- While clearing the room, the player discovers a book hidden among [[Basil]]’s possessions.

- The book has nothing immediately to do with [[Elias]]’s execution, but its presence suggests that [[Basil]] had interests the temple would not approve of.

- This begins a separate questline.

- When the player returns, [[Nestor]] provides payment and then asks for something less mundane.

- [[Elias]], the executed man, had a wife named [[Mara]].

- She disappeared shortly after her husband was arrested.

- The temple guards searched their home but found nothing useful. Nestor fears that Mara was involved in [[Elias]]’s forbidden practices and may either be in danger or continuing whatever her husband started.

- However, [[Nestor]] does not want guards marching from house to house.

- [[Elias]] had friends in the village, and after the execution many of them are unwilling to speak openly with temple authorities.

- The player is an outsider.

- That makes them useful.

- Nestor asks the player to quietly discover where Mara went.

- Questioning villagers reveals conflicting accounts of [[Elias]].

- Some describe him as increasingly obsessive before his arrest. He stopped working regularly. He wandered outside the village at night. He asked strange questions about the foundations of the temple.

- Others insist he was harmless.

- One person provides something concrete.

- A meteorologist named [[Solomon]] has been staying in the village while studying the surrounding landscape.

- Several centuries ago, something enormous struck the cliffs nearby. The impact left a partially eroded crater. [[Solomon]] believes fragments of the object may still be buried beneath the surrounding rock.

- During one of his surveys, [[Solomon]] saw [[Elias]] and [[Mara]] heading toward the cliffs.

- He thought little of it at the time.

- After [[Elias]]'s arrest, however, he saw [[Mara]] make the same journey alone.

- The player follows the route.

- Hidden among the cliffs is a narrow passage leading into an old excavation.

- Inside are signs of habitation: candles, bedding, food, carved symbols and fragments of dark, glasslike stone.

- Then people emerge from the darkness.

- They are armed.

- [[Mara]] is among them.

- These are the people the temple calls worshippers.

- Their leader orders the player to leave.

- The confrontation can end violently, but the player can instead lower their weapon and ask to speak.

- If the player cooperates, [[Mara]] tells [[Elias]]’s side of the story.

- The group does not consider itself a cult.

- They call themselves the Keepers of the Fallen Star.

- According to them, the temple's official history is a lie.

- Long before the village existed, something fell from the sky and struck the cliffs. Generations later, people began experiencing unusual dreams near fragments of the fallen object.

- Some believed the dreams were divine.

- Eventually a religious order formed around the phenomenon.

- That order became the temple.

- But centuries ago, the temple officially declared the meteorite fragments unclean and prohibited anyone from possessing them.

- The Keepers believe this was not because the fragments were evil.

- It was because the temple wanted exclusive control over them.

- [[Elias]] became involved accidentally.

- As a stonemason, he had been hired to repair part of the temple's lower foundations.

- During the work he discovered a sealed chamber.

- Inside were old carvings depicting a star falling from the heavens.

- More disturbing was their age.

- The carvings predated the oldest accepted records of the temple.

- [[Elias]] secretly copied some of them before the chamber was sealed again.

- He began investigating.

- That investigation eventually brought him to [[Mara]] and the Keepers.

- The Keepers showed [[Elias]] their most important discovery.

- The crater visible from the surface is not the true center of the impact.

- According to their excavations, the object struck at an angle and buried its largest surviving fragment deep underground.

- The village temple was later constructed almost directly above it.

- [[Elias]] became convinced that some of the temple's supposed miracles throughout history were actually connected to whatever lies beneath it.

- He intended to expose this publicly.

- Someone informed the priests.

- [[Elias]] was arrested before he could.

- [[Mara]] insists that [[Elias]] never practiced the dark rites for which he was executed.

- But she admits that the Keepers have performed rituals involving meteorite fragments.

- They sleep beside them.

- They meditate around them.

- Some claim to hear voices.

- Others experience identical dreams despite never speaking to one another.

- Mara herself has begun dreaming of a vast dark place beneath the earth.

- Something is moving there.

- She does not know whether it is a god, a creature, or simply the effect of the stone on the mind.

- The player can accuse them of being exactly what the temple claims.

- [[Mara]] does not deny the danger.

- Instead, she gives the player a problem.

- [[Elias]] hid his copied inscriptions before his arrest.

- The Keepers know roughly where: an abandoned quarry tunnel near the crater.

- But something has recently driven them out of that area.

- Animals have begun behaving strangely around exposed meteorite fragments. Several Keepers who entered the tunnel became disoriented, violent, or simply disappeared.

- Mara asks the player to recover [[Elias]]'s notes.

- This becomes the cooperation route.

- If the player confronts the Keepers instead, they refuse to surrender [[Mara]].

- The confrontation becomes violent.

- The player can kill or drive off the group and bring [[Mara]] back to the temple, or return with evidence of their location.

- Either way, the player eventually gains access to [[Elias]]'s hidden notes through the temple's investigation.

- Thus both paths converge on the same central discovery while producing different relationships and consequences.

- [[Elias]]'s notes contain sketches of the carvings beneath the temple.

- Most are impossible to understand.

- One symbol, however, appears repeatedly.

- It resembles a narrow blade.

- The player looks at the mysterious dagger received upon leaving prison.

- The same symbol is engraved near its hilt.

- For the first time, the events surrounding the player's release appear connected to what is happening in [[NIDAROS]].

- Someone knew the player would come here.

- Or wanted them to.

- And [[Elias]]'s final words begin to make sense:

- “You pray to the scar and call it a wound.”

- The temple was not built where the meteorite fell by coincidence.

- It was built to contain—or conceal—something underneath it.

- And someone powerful enough to arrange the player's release from prison has just placed the key to that mystery in the player's hand.
