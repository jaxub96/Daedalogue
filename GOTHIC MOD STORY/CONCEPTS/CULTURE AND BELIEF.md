
# Burial
- Shamans are buried in tombs with funeral masks depicting their most valuable quality.
- The mask also covers the true face of a shaman, covering their human imperfections, shames and weaknesses
- It is believed that masks keep their spirit sealed and at peace.  
- Removing a mask from a dead body is sacrilegious. 

# Funeral Masks
- A wooden mask used for burial of shamans.

# Shaman
