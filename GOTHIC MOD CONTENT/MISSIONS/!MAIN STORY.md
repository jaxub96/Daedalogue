# Chapter 1

## **[[PRISON]]**

- [[HERO]] wakes up in a prison cell.
- guard *[[Dalio]]* comes to your cell.
- choose face, head, body, name
- exit prison dungeon.
- [[Rezus]] is a neighbour prisoner.
- Calls you out when you exit. Asks if you can vouch for his release
	- Yes, I will try
		- +1 to [[MORALITY]]
		>[!TIP] [[VOUCH_REZUS]]
	- No, I don't know you
		- -1 to [[MORALITY]]
- Dalio takes you to commander [[Malcolm]]
- Malcolm signs you off, gives back your inventory
- get a [[dagger]], some gold and a [[mysterious note]]


> [!TIP] [[STRANGE_DAGGER]]



- [[mysterious note]] is mysterious and intriguing 
- ask Malcolm about items:
	- mine (lie)
		- -1 to [[MORALITY]]
		- you claim the items
	- not mine (true)
		- +1 to [[MORALITY]]
		- ask about items
		- Malcolm says they are under your name
		- not his business.
		- sell dagger, or donate to Temple
- *[[Malcolm]]* directs you to a nearby **[[SICCA_TEMPLE]]** for work/shelter
## **[[ROAD]]**
- A robber *[[Persius]]* stops you, and tries to rob you of the [[dagger]]
    - give dagger
	    - +1 to [[MORALITY]]
        - the robber takes your dagger
        - you will have to recover it later
    - fight back
	    - -1 to [[MORALITY]]
        - kill Persius
        - find a [[clue]] on his body
> [!NOTE] [[STRANGE_DAGGER]]

- walk the road from **[[PRISON]]**
- eventually find **[[SICCA_TEMPLE]]**
- temple guard *[[Varus]]* stops you
>[!TIP] [[TEMPLE_ENTRY]]

## **[[SICCA_TEMPLE]]**

- *[[Nestor]]* asks you of your background
- gives you some work in the temple
>[!TIP] [[TEMPLE_WORK]]
- gives you a room for a night.

Offers you [[NOVICE_TRAINING]]


tell him about dagger

he tells you some lore

assigns [[CULT_DUNGEON]] if not done.

Dagger has to be returned to the **[[SANCTUARY]]**

Sends you to the **[[CITY]]** to be recruited to the custodian volunteers



## **[[CITY]]**
the custodians are looking for volunteer workers to hunt the cultists
you can join if you're strong enough
go for the first expedition and kill some cultists.
their leader is a mask owner.
you take the mask
a demon appears and kills your expedition 
you return to give the news.


## **[[SANCTUARY]]**

to enter you need five masks
each mask has a power
you need to find mask-owners and kill them
return the dagger to the sanctuary
